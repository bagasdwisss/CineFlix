Synopsis

*Titanic* is a romantic drama and disaster film directed by James Cameron, set aboard the ill-fated maiden voyage of the RMS Titanic in 1912. The story follows Jack Dawson, a penniless artist, and Rose DeWitt Bukater, a young woman from a wealthy family, who meet and fall in love aboard the luxurious ship.

Despite their vastly different social backgrounds, Jack and Rose form a deep and passionate connection during their short time together. As their love blossoms, tragedy strikes when the Titanic hits an iceberg and begins to sink into the icy waters of the North Atlantic Ocean.

*Titanic* explores themes of love, sacrifice, and class division. With stunning visuals, emotional storytelling, and memorable performances, the film captivated audiences around the world and became one of the highest-grossing films of all time.
