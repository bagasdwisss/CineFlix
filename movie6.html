Synopsis

*Avengers: Infinity War* is a superhero film directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. It is the nineteenth film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), bringing together heroes from across the franchise to face their greatest threat yet: Thanos.

The film follows the Avengers and their allies as they try to stop Thanos, a powerful warlord from Titan, from collecting all six Infinity Stones. With these stones, Thanos intends to wipe out half of all life in the universe to bring what he believes is balance.

As battles rage across the cosmos—from Wakanda to Titan—heroes like Iron Man, Thor, Captain America, Doctor Strange, Spider-Man, and the Guardians of the Galaxy fight to prevent universal destruction. The movie ends on a shocking note, with Thanos succeeding and half of the universe's population disintegrating into dust.

With its high stakes, epic scale, and emotional depth, *Infinity War* is a pivotal installment in the MCU, setting the stage for the climactic *Avengers: Endgame*.
