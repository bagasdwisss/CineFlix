Sinopsis

Avengers: Endgame is a superhero film produced by Marvel Studios and directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. It serves as the epic conclusion to over a decade of interconnected films in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Set after the devastating events of Avengers: Infinity War, where the villain Thanos wiped out half of all life in the universe, the remaining Avengers and their allies must come together for one final stand.

As they grapple with loss and despair, the heroes devise a daring plan to travel through time, retrieve the Infinity Stones, and undo the destruction caused by Thanos. The journey leads to emotional reunions, intense battles, and the ultimate sacrifice that defines the legacy of Earth’s mightiest heroes.

Packed with action, emotion, and nostalgia, Avengers: Endgame delivers a powerful farewell to some of the most beloved characters in cinematic history, while celebrating the strength of unity and hope.
